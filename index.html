Why it matters

One feed. One set of signals. Every Google surface.

Shopping and Performance Max campaigns can put your products in front of people across Search, YouTube, Display, Gmail, Discover and Maps. Every one of those placements draws on the same two inputs: your product feed and your conversion data.

Get those right and everything downstream improves, from what Google bids to which products it shows and who it shows them to.
